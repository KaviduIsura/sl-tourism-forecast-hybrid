## 1. Executive Summary & Research Contribution

Phase 6 implements the **central research contribution of this dissertation**: the **Hybrid LSTM-ML Stacking Ensemble Model**.

Classical econometrics (SARIMA, Phase 3) fail during black-swan events because seasonal autoregression breaks down (86.96% shock MAPE). While Deep Learning (LSTM, Phase 4) demonstrates improved shock resilience by ingesting multi-variate sequences (58.00% shock MAPE), deep networks alone can suffer from variance inflation on small monthly samples and struggle with sharp non-linear regime shifts.

Our proposed solution is a **two-level stacking ensemble**:
1. **Level-0 Base Sequence Model**: The trained LSTM from Phase 4 generates multi-horizon trend and sequence predictions (`lstm_pred`).
2. **Level-1 Meta-Learner**: The Phase 5 candidate winner (**Random Forest**) takes a 12-dimensional hybrid matrix ($11\text{ original exogenous/lag features} + \text{lstm\_pred}$) and learns a non-linear mapping that dynamically adjusts the LSTM forecast based on real-time shock indicators.

## 2. Mathematical Formulation of the Hybrid Architecture

Let $\mathbf{x}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{11}$ represent the exogenous and autoregressive feature vector at month $t$:
$$\mathbf{x}_t = [\text{usd\_lkr}_t, \text{flights}_t, \text{hotels}_t, \text{visa}_t, \text{shock}_t, \sin_t, \cos_t, \text{lag}_1, \text{lag}_3, \text{lag}_{12}, \text{roll}_3]^T$$

### Level 0: Sequence Ingestion
The deep recurrent network maps the preceding 12-month sequence tensor into a base estimate:
$$\hat{y}_t^{\text{LSTM}} = f_{\text{LSTM}}(\mathbf{X}_{t-12:t-1}; \mathbf{\Theta}_{\text{LSTM}})$$

### Level 1: Meta-Learning on Hybrid Space
The input to the meta-learner is augmented with the LSTM prediction to form the 12-dimensional vector $\mathbf{z}_t$:
$$\mathbf{z}_t = \left[ \mathbf{x}_t^T, \; \hat{y}_t^{\text{LSTM}} \right]^T \in \mathbb{R}^{12}$$

The final prediction is generated by the ensemble of $B$ bagged decision trees:
$$\hat{y}_t^{\text{Hybrid}} = \frac{1}{B} \sum_{b=1}^{B} T_b(\mathbf{z}_t; \mathbf{\Theta}_b)$$

Where each decision tree $T_b$ can split on both tabular features (e.g. `shock_flag`, `gtrends_flights`, `usd_lkr_rate`) and the sequence prediction `lstm_pred`. This enables the trees to formulate conditional decision rules such as:
$$\text{IF } \text{shock\_flag} = 1 \text{ AND } \text{gtrends\_flights} < \tau \implies \hat{y}_t = \alpha \cdot \hat{y}_t^{\text{LSTM}} + \beta$$
Effectively correcting and dampening the LSTM output during crises.

## 5. Core Dissertation Insights: Why the Hybrid Model Triumphed

### 1. Massive Shock Error Reduction (The Breakthrough)
- In the **2022 Economic Crisis**, forecasting errors evolved as follows:
  - **SARIMA**: $86.96\%$ MAPE | $R^2 = -1.248$
  - **LSTM**: $58.00\%$ MAPE | $R^2 = -0.694$
  - **Hybrid Ensemble**: $\mathbf{31.24\%}$ **MAPE** | $\mathbf{R^2 = +0.601}$
- The proposed Hybrid Ensemble slashed shock MAPE by **55.72 percentage points** compared to SARIMA and **26.76 percentage points** compared to LSTM.
- RMSE was cut by more than half (**16,841 vs 39,996**).
- Most importantly, while both standalone models produced negative $R^2$ values during the shock, the Hybrid Ensemble achieved a strong positive $\mathbf{R^2 = +0.601}$, demonstrating that its predictions explain $60.1\%$ of the variance even during an unprecedented economic crisis.

### 2. Comprehensive Outperformance Across the Full Horizon
- Over all 48 test months (2022–2025):
  - Overall MAPE dropped to **25.79%** (down from 38.68% for SARIMA and 34.43% for LSTM).
  - Overall RMSE dropped to **43,454.4** (the lowest error in the project).
  - Overall $R^2$ jumped to **+0.533** (up from +0.361 for SARIMA and +0.262 for LSTM).

### 3. The Theoretical Advantage of Stacking
- **The LSTM provided temporal momentum**: It captured multi-step lookback dynamics and learned smooth macro recovery trends.
- **The Random Forest meta-learner provided regime-switching logic**: Decision tree thresholds on `shock_flag`, `gtrends_flights`, and `usd_lkr_rate` acted as a "circuit breaker," discounting the LSTM's trend and adjusting the forecast downwards whenever crisis conditions were active.
